# Open decisions

Status: end of Phase 0, increment 12 (2026-09-23). Every open decision of
`docs/plans/phase0-plan.md` section 19 with its current answer, the design notes increment 4 carried
here, the questions the facts sheets and the build log raised, the schema decisions increment 3
left open, ADR 0008's open Phase 1 decisions, and the ones increment 12 itself leaves. Status is **Decided** (answered and built), **Open**
(needs the owner or a measurement; the recommendation stands until then) or **Deferred** (belongs to
a later phase and blocks nothing in Phase 0). "When" is the latest point the answer is needed.
