# R3: Airport boards and route search (facts sheet)

Checked 2026-09-30 against the providers' own pages, specs, licences and terms. Repository at `main` 3302d39, read only. Local copies of every source used for audit (both live OpenAPI files, the AeroAPI licence PDFs, the FlightAware Terms and Conditions PDF, the AeroDataBox terms and pricing text) were kept outside the repository; every fact carries its URL.

Abbreviations: ADB = AeroDataBox, FIDS = ADB's airport departures and arrivals endpoints, FA = FlightAware.

## 1. Questions answered

1. **ADB host and marketplace:** the adapter targets the direct gateway `https://api.aerodatabox.com/` with the `X-Api-Key` header, so the direct Starter, Growth and Scale plans apply (not RapidAPI or API.Market); today's live direct spec is byte-identical to the vendored 1.15.3.0.
2. **FIDS maximum window:** the plan's FIDS "page size": 12 h Starter, 24 h Growth, 48 h Scale, returned in one unpaginated response.
3. **FIDS parameters:** `direction` (Arrival, Departure, Both; default Both), `withLeg` (default false), `withCancelled` (true), `withCodeshared` (true), `withCargo` (true), `withPrivate` (true), `withLocation` (false); the relative variant takes `offsetMinutes` (default -120) and `durationMinutes` (default 720).
4. **FIDS board fields:** 13-value status enum, scheduled, revised and runway times, terminal, gate, check-in desk, baggage belt, runway, per-row `codeshareStatus`, aircraft registration and model, airline; no codeshare sibling list and no `lastUpdatedUtc`.
5. **Tier and units:** TIER 2, 2 units per request, whatever the direction or window length.
6. **Growth quota and cost per refresh:** 400,000 units for $99, so one refresh is $0.000495 at list; the marginal cash cost is $0 until the quota is spent, after which calls are rejected (no cash overage on direct plans).
7. **ADB rate limit:** 10 requests per second on Growth (5 Starter, 20 Scale).
8. **Growth caching and redistribution:** cache for any duration while subscribed, but Terms 5.5 still require minimising and deleting once the purpose is served, and 10.7 requires deletion on cancellation; boards inside the app are End Use; a feed, dataset, public API or anything usable as a substitute for ADB is not.
9. **AeroAPI board endpoints:** `scheduled_departures` (selects on `scheduled_off`), `scheduled_arrivals` (`estimated_on`), `departures` (`actual_off`), `arrivals` (`actual_on`), and `/airports/{id}/flights` (all four); `start` and `end` are bounded to 10 days back and 2 days ahead.
10. **AeroAPI pagination and price:** 15 records per result set, billed per page returned; $0.005 per set for each variant, $0.020 per set for the combined endpoint.
11. **AeroAPI filters:** `airline` or `type` (General_Aviation, Airline), never both; time via `start` and `end`; `max_pages` defaults to 1.
12. **AeroAPI freshness:** no latency figure is published; an uptime guarantee exists only on Premium.
13. **AeroAPI `/schedules`:** origin, destination, airline, flight number, codeshare and regional flags; from 3 months back to 1 year ahead, span at most 3 weeks; $0.020 per 15 records; schedule data only (no status, no gates).
14. **AeroAPI caching and display terms:** Standard allows a B2C app; raw data may be kept at most 30 days under the licence but the September 2026 Terms and Conditions default to 24 hours unless the Order allows more; no use together with another real-time provider's data, and no use with any AI system, without FA's written permission.
15. **Route search by route and date:** ADB FIDS departures at the origin filtered by destination, about $0.001 per uncached search on Growth and shared with the board cache; AeroAPI `/schedules` costs about $0.02 to $0.14 per search.
16. **Recommendation:** ADB FIDS only, fetched as 12 h airport-local buckets with `direction=Both`, coalesced in the AirportState object with KV as the read path, TTL by distance from now, filters applied server-side after the cache; incremental cost is $0 at 1k and 10k and about $24 of Cloudflare at 100k in the worst case, with ADB quota as the real constraint.

- **C3. - **C4. - **C7. - **C10. Stale window figure.** `docs/research/phase0-dossier.md:301` gives FIDS a 12 h window; that is Starter's. Growth is 24 h, as `config.ts:38-44` already has.
- **C11. Growth caching summary.** `docs/increments/06-07-providers-and-trackers.facts.md:125` reads Growth as unlimited while subscribed; Terms 5.5 (updated 2026-09-19) keep the minimise and delete-when-served duties (F13), so board caches must be purged, never archived.

## 4. Design implications for Phase 1

- **D1. Provider: ADB FIDS only for boards and route search.** No AeroAPI fallback. *Trade-off:* schedules-only airports show no live status or gates, and ADB's coverage outside the US and EU is uneven (F10), whereas AeroAPI would show FA's global tracking. In exchange: 100x to 700x cheaper per refresh (section 5), no competition with tracker polls for AeroAPI's 5 sets/s, and no dependence on FA's item 10 permission (C1).
- **D2. One fetch shape per call:** `direction=Both`, `withLeg=true`, `withCodeshared=true`, `withCancelled=true`, `withCargo=false`, `withPrivate=false`, `withLocation=false`. *Trade-off:* larger payloads (both directions, both legs, codeshare duplicates) for half the calls, plus one cache that serves both boards and route search. Measure payload size first (U4).
- **D3. Buckets: 12 h, aligned to airport-local 00:00 to 11:59 and 12:00 to 23:59.** *Trade-off against 24 h buckets on Growth:* route search costs 2 calls per date instead of 1, and views near noon straddle two buckets. In exchange, each "now" refresh downloads half as much, the scheme works on the Starter development key, and a normalised bucket stays well under the Durable Object's 2 MB value cap (F37).
- **D4. Coalescing and cache: AirportState per ICAO is the only caller of FIDS.** It keeps an in-flight `Map` keyed by bucket (the `#inflight` pattern of `designator-resolver.ts:171, 220-229`), required because outbound fetch lets requests interleave (F37). It stores each bucket as one compressed blob, not a row per flight (at an assumed 150 rows per refresh, 1,437,500 refreshes x 150 = 216M rows written, about $216 a month at 100k once the flight layer has used the 50M included), and writes KV `board:v2:{ICAO}:{bucketStartLocal}` carrying `fetchedAt`, `freshUntil` and `staleUntil`. Workers read KV first (`cacheTtl` 30 s) and call the object only when the entry is missing or past `freshUntil`. N concurrent viewers therefore cost one provider call per bucket per TTL. *Trade-off:* KV reads can be up to about 60 s behind and writes cost $5 per million; a read path through the object alone is exact but adds a cross-region hop to every view.
- **D5. TTL ladder by bucket position, with stale-while-revalidate:**

  | Bucket position | Fresh for | Serve stale until | Why |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | contains now, or starts within 3 h | 5 min | 15 min | live layer; same as the repo's 300 s and FA's sample default (F35) |
  | starts 3 h to 24 h ahead | 30 min | 2 h | estimates rarely move this early |
  | starts 24 h to 72 h ahead | 3 h | 12 h | mostly the schedules layer |
  | starts more than 72 h ahead | 12 h | 48 h | schedules refresh every 2 weeks (F10) |
  | ended less than 3 h ago | 15 min | 1 h | actual times settle |
  | ended more than 3 h ago | 6 h, no refresh after 24 h | purge 48 h after the bucket ends | Terms 5.5 minimisation (F13) |

  *Trade-off:* staleness against quota. The ladder is also the degrade lever in D9.
- **D6. Coverage pre-check.** Call the free health endpoint (F11) once per airport per day (KV `adb:coverage:{ICAO}`), skip FIDS where neither schedules nor live is covered (avoiding billed 204s), and badge schedule-only airports. *Trade-off:* one extra free call per airport per day and a coverage flag up to a day old.
- **D7. Group codeshares server-side.** Collapse rows sharing scheduled UTC time, opposite airport, and registration or callsign; the `IsOperator` row is primary and the others fill `codeshares[]`. *Trade-off:* heuristic errors at airports that report `Unknown`; the alternative `withCodeshared=false` drops the marketing numbers users search by.
- **D8. Filters server-side, after the cache.** The cache key never includes filters. The Worker trims the bucket(s) to the requested time range and airline (marketing or operating, including grouped codeshares) and returns a UI-shaped payload with an ETag; the client refines within that window (status, hide codeshares) without a round trip. *Trade-off:* trivial Worker CPU per request, against shipping a full 12 h hub board (hundreds of KB) to a phone for client-only filtering.
- **D9. Budget and attribution.** Add `board` and `route_search` triggers and an `airport_icao` column to `provider_calls` (C12). Reserve a boards sub-budget inside ProviderBudget, say 35% of the daily ADB cap. At 70% of it double the TTLs, at 90% quadruple them, at 100% serve stale only. *Trade-off:* boards go stale on heavy days, but trackers never starve (C13).
- **D10. Route search.** `GET /v1/flights/search?origin=&destination=&date=` reads the origin's two buckets for that date through AirportState, filters departures by destination (one lookup per airport for multi-airport cities), and returns grouped candidates; adding one goes through the existing DesignatorResolver (one 2-unit status call). *Trade-off:* limited to ADB schedule coverage and lookahead (365 days on Growth), no connections, codeshare heuristics; AeroAPI `/schedules` gives airline-published schedules but costs 20x to 140x more per search and needs FA's permission (C1).
- **D11. Access and licence posture for boards.** Require a session (anonymous accounts allowed), cap board fetches per principal (for example 60 an hour), return UI-shaped rows only (no raw provider JSON), and keep boards out of share pages, public API tokens and MCP until ADB confirms (U9). *Trade-off:* some friction and no public SEO boards. It is needed because an open JSON endpoint for any airport could be judged a feed or substitute for ADB (F14, F15).
- **D12. AeroAPI item 10 gates the Phase 1 architecture.** If FA refuses permission the options are: (a) ADB only, meaning cadence B or today's mock mode; (b) AeroAPI only, with pre-48 h refreshes through `/schedules` at $0.020 and boards at the AeroAPI prices in section 5; (c) a negotiated Order. *Trade-off:* (a) caps quality at ADB's live coverage; (b) multiplies provider cost; (c) takes time.
- **D13. Freshness display.** FIDS rows carry no `lastUpdatedUtc` (F7), so show the bucket's `fetchedAt` ("as of"). `revisedTime` may be a gate or runway time (F8), so label estimates cautiously. *Trade-off:* honesty against visual clutter.
- **D14. No pre-warming except tracked airports.** Never sweep all airports (Terms 8.2, F17). Pre-fetch only the origin and destination buckets of tracked flights inside 24 h, which ties board cost to unique flights and airports as the brief requires. *Trade-off:* the first view of an untracked airport pays a cold miss (an unmeasured latency, U4).

## 5. Cost numbers

**Assumptions.** A1: MAU 600 / 7,500 / 125,000 at 1k / 10k / 100k flights (`docs/cost-estimate.md` section 4). A2: **6 board views per MAU per month** (about 2 tracked flights per MAU, giving an origin departures board and a destination arrivals board each, plus 2 browsing views). A3: **2 route searches per MAU per month**. A4: with D3's 12 h buckets and `Both`, a view that misses the cache costs 1.25 FIDS calls (a quarter straddle two buckets) and a search that misses costs 2 calls. A5: **worst case, no sharing:** every view and search misses; sharing only lowers the numbers. A6: 2 units per FIDS call (F3). A7: the flight layer uses 8 ADB units per flight with AeroAPI live and 156 in mock mode (`docs/cost-estimate.md:67-71`).

**Per call.** Growth: $99 / 400,000 = $0.0002475 per unit, so one refresh (2 units) is **$0.000495** and one uncached route search (4 units) is **$0.00099**. Scale: $499 / 4,000,000 = $0.00012475 per unit, so a refresh is $0.00025. Direct plans charge nothing extra until the quota is spent, then refuse calls (F5); the real price of a refresh is quota.

**Quota ceiling for live boards.** Growth: 400,000 / 2 = 200,000 calls a month, or 6,667 a day. One bucket refreshed every 5 minutes for 24 h costs 288 calls, so 6,667 / 288 = about **23 airports** kept live around the clock would exhaust Growth alone; after the 10k flight layer (80,000 units = 40,000 calls a month = 1,333 a day) about 18 remain. Scale: 4,000,000 / 2 / 30 = 66,667 calls a day, minus 13,333 for the 100k flight layer, leaves about 185 airports. This is why D5 and D9 exist.
